Use the attached house photos as the reference. Work in two stages: complete Stage 1 only, then wait for my answers to any questions and my explicit approval before starting Stage 2.

## Stage 1: Analyze the house

Analyze the attached photos of my house. Do NOT generate any image yet. Write a precise, numbered HOUSE SHEET with:

1. Overall shape and footprint, number of storeys, attached buildings.
2. Roof: type, number of slopes, material, color, chimneys, skylights.
3. Walls: materials and colors per facade.
4. For EACH visible facade: number and position of windows (left to right, per floor), doors, shutters type (roller / swing / none), their color.
5. Garage: number of doors, door type (sectional / roller / swing), position.
6. Plot: gate type (sliding / swing / none), fences, low walls, driveway, paths, terrace, pool, pergola, awning, garden elements, trees.
7. Every exterior light fixture you can see and its exact position.
8. Anything unclear or not visible in the photos: list it as a question.

## Stage 2: Generate the diorama after approval

Transform the attached photo of my house into a highly detailed 3D isometric miniature diorama.

FIDELITY: keep the exact architecture, proportions, roof shape, number and position of windows, doors and garage doors, and the surrounding elements (driveway, garden, fences, trees). Do not add or remove any building element.

CAMERA: true isometric view, front three-quarter angle, the whole property visible and centered, nothing cropped, generous empty margin around the model.

STYLE: clean stylized 3D render, soft global illumination, smooth matte materials, subtle ambient occlusion, premium architectural model look.

LIGHTING: bright sunny day, soft shadows. All exterior and interior lights OFF. All garage doors, gates and shutters CLOSED.

BACKGROUND: plain uniform dark background (#1C1C1C), no sky, no horizon. The diorama sits on a thin square ground base with clean edges.

NO text, NO labels, NO people, NO cars, NO watermark. Landscape 16:9, maximum resolution.
