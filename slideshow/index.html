      How it works

      Each deck is exported from PowerPoint to lightweight images, so it opens instantly on any device without Office or a download.

      • 🎮 Play your wayArrows, space bar, mouse clicks on the slide edges or a swipe on mobile. Use the chapter bar under the slide to jump ahead.
      • 🔗 Share a slideThe address bar always points to the current deck and slide, so you can send a link to the exact slide you want to discuss.
      • ✅ Pick up where you left offSeen slides are ticked and the last position is saved in your browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
      • ♿ AccessibleEvery slide comes with its text, read aloud by screen readers and searchable from the deck search box.
